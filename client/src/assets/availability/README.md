# Availability hero photo

`hero.jpg` is the image used by the team's MH-52 mockup, sourced from
`docs/mockups/availability/hero.jpg` and resized to 1600 × 994 pixels, then
JPEG-compressed for the client (approximately 219 KB, down from 574 KB).

Photo: Wolfgang Moroder, *Sangon Hut with the Odles Group and the Mastlé Mountain*.
Source: https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Sangon_utia_da_mont_sun_Ciaulonch_cun_Odles_y_stevia.jpg
License: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/3.0/

The photo is a stand-in for the hut, as in the mockup. Its author, source, licence
and resizing/compression notice are visible in the landing-page footer.
